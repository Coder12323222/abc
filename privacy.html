Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Clipping HQ is a creator workflow that helps users discover public story leads, prepare original content, create animated vertical videos, and publish creator-approved videos to supported social platforms.

YouTube API Services

Clipping HQ uses YouTube API Services. Use of Clipping HQ's YouTube features is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

YouTube data we access

When a user connects YouTube through Google OAuth 2.0, Clipping HQ may receive OAuth access and refresh tokens, the connected channel ID, channel title, and the authorization scopes granted by the user. Clipping HQ uses the YouTube Data API channels.list method to verify the connected channel and the videos.insert method to upload a video selected and approved by the user.

Clipping HQ requests the youtube.readonly and youtube.upload scopes. It does not use the YouTube Data API to download videos, scrape YouTube pages, sell YouTube data, provide advertising services, or publish without a user action.

How YouTube data is used and shared

YouTube data is used only to connect the user's channel, confirm upload authorization, create a secure resumable upload session, transfer the user's selected video to YouTube, and show the resulting channel and upload status. Uploaded video files are sent directly from the user's browser to YouTube and are not retained by Clipping HQ's server. Clipping HQ does not sell YouTube data or share it with independent advertisers or data brokers.

Storage and security

YouTube OAuth credentials and connected-channel identifiers are encrypted before being placed in a Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite cookie associated with the user's browser. Clipping HQ does not maintain a centralized server database of YouTube profiles or uploaded video files. The connection cookie is retained for no longer than 180 days unless refreshed to keep a user-requested connection active, or until the user disconnects or revokes access.

Disconnecting, revocation, and deletion

A user may disconnect YouTube from the Accounts screen in Clipping HQ. Clipping HQ then clears the local connection cookie immediately and attempts to revoke the associated Google OAuth token. A user may also revoke Clipping HQ directly from Google Account permissions.

Because Clipping HQ does not maintain a centralized database of YouTube user data, clearing or revoking the connection removes the YouTube data held by Clipping HQ for that browser. Any additional server-held personal information identified in a verified deletion request will be deleted within seven days, unless retention is required by law or for security and fraud prevention.

Other information we process

Clipping HQ may process content ideas, titles, scripts, captions, uploaded media selected by the user, and limited diagnostic information needed to provide requested features, improve reliability, and protect the service from misuse. Drafts and approval-queue information are primarily stored in the user's browser.

AI and other service providers

Some features may send limited content context to service providers used for script generation, narration, licensed visual sourcing, hosting, and application operation. These providers process information only to deliver the requested feature. Clipping HQ does not sell personal information.

User choices

Users choose whether to connect a social account and initiate every upload. Users are responsible for reviewing generated material, metadata, rights, and privacy settings before publishing. Browser-stored drafts can be removed by deleting them in Clipping HQ or clearing the site's browser data.

Children

Clipping HQ is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision.

Contact and deletion requests

For privacy questions or verified deletion requests, contact the Clipping HQ operator using the primary contact address provided in the application's official platform listings or compliance submissions.
