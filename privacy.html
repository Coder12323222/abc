Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Clipping HQ is a content workflow tool that helps users discover content opportunities, prepare original post ideas, and connect supported social media accounts.

Information we process

When you use Clipping HQ, we may process information you choose to provide, such as saved content ideas and account authorization data. When you connect a supported social platform through OAuth, we may receive basic profile or channel information and access tokens needed to provide the features you authorize.

How we use information

We use information only to operate Clipping HQ, provide requested features, maintain account connections, prepare content workflows, improve reliability, and protect the service from misuse.

Social platform data

Clipping HQ does not ask for or store your social media passwords. Supported account connections use official OAuth authorization. Tokens and permissions are used only for the functions you approve and may be revoked through the relevant platform.

AI and third-party services

Some features may send limited content context to third-party services used to generate drafts or operate the application. We do not sell personal information.

Data retention and security

We retain information only as reasonably necessary to provide the service. We use technical safeguards intended to protect stored credentials and authorization tokens, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may disconnect linked social accounts through the relevant platform and stop using Clipping HQ at any time. You may also request deletion of information associated with the service by contacting the operator.

Children

Clipping HQ is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. The updated date above will reflect the latest version.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the operator through the contact method provided for Clipping HQ.
